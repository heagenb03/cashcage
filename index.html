Two ways to settle

Direct settles player-to-player in the fewest transfers using math, not a naive everyone-pays-everyone split and no overly complex AI. Or name a banker and the whole table pays out like in a real table.

Paid how they like

Every player sets how they get paid with Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Apple Cash, Zelle, or cash. Pay from the app or share the summary

Amounts that match the preferred rounding

Pick the night’s rounding preference from $1 to $50 (or exact) and settlements come out in amounts people can actually hand over.

Regulars saved

Save your usual players with their payment info once with syncing across your devices.

Made for the host

Built for whoever’s running the night and the friends who just want to know what they owe.

How it works

  1. Start a game and add tonight’s players.
  2. Log buy-ins and cash-outs as the night runs.
  3. Tap Complete Game — Cash Cage returns the payment list: the fewest player-to-player transfers, or everyone settling up with one banker.
  4. Share the summary and everyone’s handle is right there, so payment happens in the group chat.
